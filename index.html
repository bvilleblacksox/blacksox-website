About the Black Sox

Based in Biglerville, Pennsylvania, the Biglerville Black Sox are a proud member of the South Penn Baseball League. We are a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to supporting local baseball, growing young men, and having a positive involvement in the community.
